Home

by Waves on Board · 2026

Role
Director
Form
Devised improvisational theater
Process
Four-month workshop, seven performers
Place
Abu Dhabi, UAE

An improvisational theater project about belonging, home, migration and the feeling of being away from what we know. Seven performers from different countries, generations and life experiences built a collective performance through movement, storytelling and improvisation, unfolding in real time.

The performers created characters that carry echoes of themselves. Set in a transit airport after a sudden flight disruption, the story brings strangers together until a darker truth surfaces: a hidden human trafficking chain. Will they find the waves that connect them, and a home together on board?

F28

Fireworks, Missiles, and the “Foreseeable” Future · 2026–

In progress

Role
Co-initiator, interviewer, editor
With
Ruzai (Better) Hao
Form
Oral history, independent publication
Place
Abu Dhabi, UAE

For many international students in Abu Dhabi, fireworks meant holidays, family and new years. Then the sound of missile interceptions arrived, and the same sudden burst in the sky stopped meaning one thing. F28 records that moment from the inside: what young people heard, who they called, and how they decided to stay, leave or wait.

Within a month of the events we interviewed 17 peers in Chinese and English, while we were living through the same uncertainty. The next phase invites each participant back to listen to their own words, respond to the person they were a few months earlier, and decide what is kept, changed or withdrawn.

JAM!

Short film · 2026

In progress

Role
Writer
Director
Chiara Giannantonio
Form
Short film

Emma and Frida are two musicians with completely opposite personalities, standing at the threshold of “elderly.” An unexpected reunion turns into an unlikely friendship that helps them let go of the standards they have spent a lifetime chasing, and remember what it feels like to simply be alive.

JAM! is about friendship, control and grief, about loneliness and its resolution, and above all about music and fun: a hopeful story of connection and revival.

Kids

Live coding performance · May 2026

Role
Visuals (Hydra)
With
Elora Trotter, Yihan Zhang
Course
Live Coding, NYU Abu Dhabi

An audiovisual performance about youth, wonder and the universe. Elora and Yihan built the music around a sample of kids' voices, and I performed the visuals live in Hydra, mapped to MIDI so image and sound move together.

“I thought about my own adolescent years, my own first love… me and my friends sitting around at slumber parties in the ’90s, giddy even just thinking about boys. Teenager. It packs a lot of emotion and imagery into three syllables.”

Elora Trotter

About

Yutong Tang is a theater-maker, performer and interdisciplinary artist from China, studying Theater and Computer Science at New York University Abu Dhabi.

Her work is shaped by questions of migration, memory, language and collective belonging. She treats theater as both an art form and a social space: a way to rehearse empathy, hold difference, and turn personal experience into shared expression.

Alongside her theater practice she works in design, technical production and programming, and brings that interdisciplinary eye to how stories are staged, documented and experienced.
